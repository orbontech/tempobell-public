Quick fixes

  • No sound while locked: allow Notifications for TempoBell in system Settings.
  • Pro is not unlocked: tap the TempoBell logo, then choose Restore Purchase with the Apple Account that bought it.
  • Watch is not updating: open TempoBell on both devices once and keep Bluetooth enabled.
  • No heart rate or workout: confirm Health access. The timer works without it.

Please include app version, device model, and operating-system version in technical reports. Do not email Health details or sensitive information.
