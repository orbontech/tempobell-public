PRIVACY POLICY · SEPTEMBER 4, 2026

Your training
stays yours.

TempoBell works without an account or developer-operated backend. OrbonTech does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information, routines, workout history, or Health data.

Data on your devices

Timer presets, settings, purchase-entitlement cache, and workout history are stored locally. Your phone and watch may exchange presets and workout summaries directly through Watch Connectivity. OrbonTech cannot access this information.

Health data

Health access is optional and requested only when you start a workout on your watch. With permission, TempoBell may read heart rate during that workout and save the user-initiated workout to Health.

Health data is never sent to OrbonTech or a third-party server, and is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, data mining, sale, or disclosure to third parties. The timer remains functional when access is denied.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is processed by Apple. TempoBell receives a signed entitlement confirming access; OrbonTech does not receive payment-card information.

Notifications and Live Activities

Granted notification access is used only for local timer cues. No push server is used. Active timer state is supplied locally to Live Activities.

No tracking

TempoBell contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or OrbonTech analytics backend. Apple may provide aggregated diagnostics under your device analytics settings.

Children

TempoBell is a general fitness utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Material changes will update the date above and be described in release notes. Questions or privacy requests: support@orbontech.com.
